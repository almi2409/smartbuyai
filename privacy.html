Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

SmartBuy AI collects no personal data. It reads public product information on AliExpress only when you ask it to, and stores nothing about you.

What the extension reads

SmartBuy AI runs only on aliexpress.com and aliexpress.us. When you click “Check reviews” and then “Analyze”, it reads from that product:

  • the product title and price;
  • the star rating and number of reviews;
  • the store name and its positive-feedback rate, when shown;
  • up to twenty recent public reviews (text and stars), loaded from AliExpress’s own public review service.

It does not read your account, orders, cart, addresses, payment details or browsing history.

Where that data goes

The product information above is sent once to our analysis server (a Cloudflare Worker), which asks an AI model for the verdict and sends it straight back to your browser. Nothing is saved on the server, and nothing in the request identifies you.

What stays in your browser

Two small settings are kept in Chrome’s local extension storage on your device: your chosen language and a count of how many products you have analysed. A cached copy of the current coupon list is kept there too. You can clear all of it by removing the extension.

Deals and affiliate links

The extension downloads a short list of current AliExpress coupons from smartbuyai.app. When a valid coupon exists, it shows it with an “Activate deal” button. Only if you click that button does the extension open AliExpress through our affiliate partner (Admitad), which may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are never added without your click, and the affiliate partner’s own privacy policy applies to that visit.

This website

smartbuyai.app is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics, no cookies and no tracking scripts. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

SmartBuy AI is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
