What it reads

It reads the newest twenty reviews 5 stars, weighs the stars against the words 28, checks the store Shop and shows a coupon -$5 only when one exists.

Each check takes about ten seconds. You get a verdict, a trust score from 0 to 100, the fake-review risk, the pros and cons buyers keep repeating and the red flags worth a second look.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no card. You install it from the Chrome Web Store and it works on the next product you open.

Does it work on Amazon?

No. SmartBuy AI is built for AliExpress only: its review format, its translated reviews and its stores. One shop, done properly.

Can it promise a product is good?

No tool can. It is a second opinion built from what buyers wrote. A low score means read the reviews yourself before paying; a high score means the reviews look honest.

What happens to my data?

Nothing personal is collected. Only the product title, price, star rating and public review text are sent for analysis, and nothing is stored. Details in the privacy policy.

How do you earn anything?

When a real AliExpress coupon exists, SmartBuy shows it. If you click “Activate deal”, we may earn a small commission at no cost to you. We never add an affiliate link without your click.

Which languages?

English and Serbian. It picks your browser’s language, and you can switch it in the extension’s menu.
